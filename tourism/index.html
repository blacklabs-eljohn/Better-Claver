Where Red Soil Meets the Pacific Ocean

Home of the Panaghiusa Festival, Hayanggabon Port gateway to Sohoton Cove & Bucas Grande Islands, pristine coastal islets, and rich Mamanwa cultural heritage.

Annual Municipal Festival

Panaghiusa Festival

"Panaghiusa" — Surigaonon for Unity & Cultural Pride

Annual Celebration September 9th

Celebrated every 9th of September, the Panaghiusa Festival honors Claver’s patron saint, San Pedro Calungsod, while celebrating the municipal spirit of unity ("Panaghiusa"). The festival highlights vibrant street dancing, indigenous Mamanwa cultural showcases, agricultural trade fairs, and the famous red-and-gold coastal parade.

Street Dancing Competition

Contested by schools and barangay contingents depicting Claver's history and mining heritage.

Agro-Industrial & Mining Expo

Showcasing fresh agricultural produce, local handicrafts, and sustainable mineral developments.

Mamanwa Cultural Heritage

Honoring the indigenous first inhabitants of Claver with traditional songs, dances, and weaving.

How to Get to Claver

From Surigao City (Airport/Bus Terminal):

Take a passenger van or Bachelor Express bus bound for Claver/Tandag (approx. 1.5 to 2 hours ride along the highway).

From Butuan City:

Board a bus/van at Butuan Integrated Terminal heading northeast via Surigao-Dapa highway junction to Claver.
