Disaster Preparedness Tips

  • Prepare a 72-hour emergency go-bag (water, non-perishable food, flashlight, first aid).
  • Monitor PAGASA storm advisories and coastal wave warnings before seafaring.
  • Follow MDRRMO evacuation orders immediately during heavy rainfall warnings.
  • Keep emergency hotline numbers saved on all family mobile phones.
