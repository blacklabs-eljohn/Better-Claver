Regulatory & Statutory Legal Framework

Mining operations in the Philippines are regulated under Republic Act No. 7942 (Philippine Mining Act of 1995) and supervised by the Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR) - Mines and Geosciences Bureau (MGB).

SDMP Mandate (DAO 2010-21): Mining permit holders are mandated by law to allocate 1.5% of their annual direct operating cost for Social Development and Management Programs (SDMP).
EPEP & Rehabilitation: Mandatory Environmental Protection and Enhancement Programs (EPEP) and Final Mine Rehabilitation and Decommissioning Plans (FMRDP) safeguard environmental restoration.
Local Royalty Share (LGC Sec. 290): 40% of national wealth taxes collected from mining in Claver are remitted back to local government units (LGU Claver, Province of Surigao del Norte, and host Barangays).
