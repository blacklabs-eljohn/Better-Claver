Public Infrastructure & Facilities

Community Investments & Infrastructure

Publicly funded roads, school buildings, health stations, water supply networks, and community facilities constructed through municipal mining tax revenues and company SDMP funds in Claver.

School Infrastructure

Claver National High School Science & Computer Building

Location: Poblacion, Claver
Funding Entity: TMC & THPAL Joint Fund
Budget: ₱22,000,000.00
Status: Operational (2023)

Two-storey 8-classroom facility equipped with 50 desktop computers, science lab instruments, and rooftop solar power backup system.

Disaster & Community

Hayanggabon Evacuation Center & Multi-Purpose Gym

Location: Brgy. Hayanggabon
Funding Entity: Taganito Mining Corp (TMC)
Budget: ₱31,500,000.00
Status: Operational (2022)

Typhoon-resilient multi-purpose facility capable of accommodating 1,200 evacuees with emergency standby power, kitchen, and sanitation blocks.

Road Infrastructure

Urbiztondo-Lapinigan Farm-to-Market Road Paving

Location: Brgys. Urbiztondo & Lapinigan
Funding Entity: PGMC & SMDC Joint Fund
Budget: ₱45,000,000.00
Status: Completed (2024)

Concrete paving of 6.2 kilometers of agricultural road connecting interior upland farms to national transportation routes.
