Environmental Compliance

Environmental Information & Mine Rehabilitation

Summaries of progressive mine re-vegetation, tree planting totals, water quality monitoring, and biodiversity conservation in Claver, verified by DENR-EMB and Multi-Partite Monitoring Teams (MMT).

Progressive Rehabilitation

Over 1,420 hectares of mined-out land have undergone progressive rehabilitation with 8.75 million native trees, agoho, and endemic bamboo species planted.

Rehabilitated Area: 1,420 Hectares

Water Quality Monitoring

Continuous quarterly water quality sampling at 42 active monitoring stations conducted by Multi-Partite Monitoring Teams (MMT) composed of LGU, NGO, and DENR representatives.

Monitoring Stations: 42 MMT Stations

Coastal & Marine Protection

380 hectares of mangrove reforestation and artificial reef deployment along coastal barangays to safeguard marine biodiversity in Hinadkaban Bay.

Mangrove Reforestation: 380 Hectares
