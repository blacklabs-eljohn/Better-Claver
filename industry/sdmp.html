Mandatory Social Development

Social Development & Management Program (SDMP)

Under DENR Administrative Order No. 2010-21, mining permit holders allocate 1.5% of annual operating costs for social development. Below are verified SDMP project records submitted to DENR-MGB.

Filter Category:

Barangay Taganito Multi-Purpose Tribal & Cultural Center

Infrastructure & Cultural Heritage
Operating Company: Taganito Mining Corporation (TMC)
Approved Budget: ₱18,500,000.00
Year Approved: 2024
Beneficiaries: Barangays Taganito & Hayanggabon

Construction of a modern 2-storey tribal hall and community center equipped with emergency evacuation facilities and IP council meeting chambers for indigenous cultural communities in Taganito.

Supporting Document: TMC Annual SDMP Performance Report 2024 (DENR-MGB XIII Submitted) Completed

College & Technical Vocational Scholarship Program

Education & Youth
Operating Company: Platinum Group Metals Corporation (PGMC)
Approved Budget: ₱24,000,000.00
Year Approved: 2024
Beneficiaries: Cagdianao, Hayanggabon, Urbiztondo, Lapinigan

Full tertiary tuition, monthly living allowance, book stipend, and board review assistance for 420 local students pursuing Engineering, Nursing, Agriculture, and Education degrees.

Supporting Document: PGMC SDMP Educational Accomplishment Report Q4 2024 Ongoing

Claver Coastal Barangay Water Distribution Pipeline Network

Public Infrastructure & Water
Operating Company: Taganito HPAL Nickel Corporation (THPAL)
Approved Budget: ₱35,200,000.00
Year Approved: 2023 - 2024
Beneficiaries: Taganito, Hayanggabon, Cabancaban

Installation of 12-kilometer potable water transmission pipelines, filtration tanks, and household tap connections benefiting 2,800 coastal households in eastern Claver.

Supporting Document: THPAL Community Infrastructure SDMP Turnover Record 2024 Completed

Fisherfolk Livelihood & Motorized Banka Distribution

Livelihood & Agriculture
Operating Company: CTP Construction and Mining Corp
Approved Budget: ₱8,400,000.00
Year Approved: 2024
Beneficiaries: Adlay, Dahican, Magallanes

Provision of 85 fiberglass motorized fishing boats, deep-sea fishing nets, safety equipment, and cold storage units to accredited municipal fisherfolk associations.

Supporting Document: CTP Mining Livelihood Audit Report 2024 Completed

Barangay Cagdianao Health Station & Ambulance Turnover

Health & Medical
Operating Company: Platinum Group Metals Corporation (PGMC)
Approved Budget: ₱14,800,000.00
Year Approved: 2023
Beneficiaries: Cagdianao

Construction of a fully equipped primary health station and turnover of a Type II basic life support ambulance to Barangay Cagdianao.

Supporting Document: PGMC Health & Medical Assistance SDMP Summary Completed
