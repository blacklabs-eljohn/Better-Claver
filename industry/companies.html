Company Profiles

Operating Mining Companies

Factual profiles of major mining and mineral processing companies operating in Claver, Surigao del Norte, based on DENR-MGB mining registries and official corporate disclosures.

Taganito Mining Corporation (TMC)

Subsidiary of Nickel Asia Corporation (NAC)
MPSA No. 266-2008-XIII
Commodity: Nickel Laterite Ore & Cobalt
Location: Brgy. Taganito & Hayanggabon, Claver
Operating Area: 4,860 Hectares
Years of Operation: 1987 – Present (39+ Years)
Contact: (086) 826-5000
Email: info@nickelasia.com

Taganito Mining Corporation (TMC) is one of the largest nickel mining operations in the Philippines. Operating in Claver since 1987, TMC extracts high-grade and medium-grade nickel laterite ore supplied to domestic hydrometallurgical processing (THPAL) and international export markets, while funding extensive community SDMP programs.

Source: DENR Mines and Geosciences Bureau (MGB) & NAC Disclosures
Official Website Official Careers

Taganito HPAL Nickel Corporation (THPAL)

Joint Venture: Sumitomo Metal Mining Co., Ltd. & Nickel Asia Corp
PEZA Export Processing Zone
Commodity: Mixed Nickel-Cobalt Sulfide (MS)
Location: Taganito Special Economic Zone, Claver
Plant Area: 120 Hectares Complex
Years of Operation: 2013 – Present (13 Years)
Contact: (086) 826-8888
Email: contact@thpal.com.ph

THPAL operates the Philippines' state-of-the-art High Pressure Acid Leach (HPAL) hydrometallurgical processing plant in Claver. It processes low-grade nickel limonite ore into high-value Mixed Nickel-Cobalt Sulfide used globally in EV battery manufacturing and advanced technology applications.

Source: PEZA & DENR Environmental Management Bureau (EMB)
Official Website

Platinum Group Metals Corporation (PGMC)

Subsidiary of Global Ferronickel Holdings, Inc. (FNI)
MPSA No. 007-92-X
Commodity: Nickel Laterite Ore (Saprolite & Limonite)
Location: Barangay Cagdianao, Claver
Operating Area: 4,376 Hectares
Years of Operation: 2006 – Present (20 Years)
Contact: (02) 8894-1111
Email: info@fni.com.ph

PGMC operates the Cagdianao Nickel Expansion Project in Claver. It is a major exporter of high-grade saprolite and limonite nickel ore to international smelting facilities and maintains extensive SDMP, educational scholarships, and environmental protection programs across coastal barangays.

Source: DENR-MGB Caraga Region XIII & FNI SEC Filings
Official Website Official Careers

Shenzhou Mining & Development Corporation (SMDC)

Claver Mineral Development Corporation Partner
MPSA No. 250-2007-XIII
Commodity: Nickel Ore
Location: Brgys. Urbiztondo & Magallanes, Claver
Operating Area: 2,310 Hectares
Years of Operation: 2007 – Present
Contact: (086) 326-5120
Email: info@smdc-claver.ph

SMDC holds mineral production sharing agreements in northern Claver, supplying raw nickel ore for industrial processing while contributing to local community development funds under DENR regulatory oversight.
