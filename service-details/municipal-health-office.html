Health Services

Municipal Health Office (MHO)

Official Citizen's Charter standards for outpatient medical services, maternal care, immunization, animal bite treatment, laboratory testing, sanitation permits, and health cards in Claver, Surigao del Norte.

Medical Certificate

Issuance of medical certificate for employment, school, sports, driving, and general health clearance.

Fee: Free Time: 15-30 mins

Immunization Services

Routine infant & child vaccines, COVID-19, flu, and pneumococcal vaccinations.

Fee: Free Time: 20-30 mins

Animal Bite Treatment

Rabies post-exposure prophylaxis, wound cleaning, and vaccine administration at ABTC.

Fee: Free Time: 30 mins

TB-DOTS & GeneXpert

Tuberculosis screening, sputum GeneXpert testing, and free 6-month DOTS treatment regimen.

Fee: Free Time: 20-48 hrs

Sanitary Permit

Issuance of sanitary permits for food and non-food business establishments.

Fee: Varies Time: 20 mins

Health Card (Pink Card)

Issuance of health cards for food handlers and service workers following lab clearance.

Fee: Varies Time: 30 mins
