Public Utilities

Public Utilities Regulation Office (PURO)

New municipal water service connection applications, reconnection of disconnected water lines, and transfer of water service connections.

New Water Service Connection

Application, site inspection, and installation of new municipal water service meter.

Fee: Varies (Application fee) Time: 15-30 mins

Reconnection of Water Service

Reconnection of water line upon settlement of account balances.

Fee: Reconnection Fee Time: 30 mins

Transfer of Water Service Connection

Transfer of water meter location or transfer of account ownership.

Fee: Transfer Fee Time: 2 hours 15 mins
