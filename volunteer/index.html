Civic Technology Initiative

Join Us in Building a Better Claver

BetterClaver is 100% community-powered and volunteer-driven. Lend your skills in engineering, design, content, translation, or research to make municipal information accessible for all Clavernons.

Why Join Us

Why Volunteer With BetterClaver?

Civic technology empowers citizens by turning complex public records into easy-to-use digital tools.

Real Civic Impact

Help thousands of residents find municipal service requirements, emergency hotlines, and public budget data effortlessly.

Open-Source Portfolio

Gain real-world experience collaborating on modern web development, Git workflows, accessibility standards, and product design.

Flexible & Remote

Contribute asynchronously from anywhere at your own pace — whether 1 hour a week or taking lead on a project feature.

Open Roles

Where You Can Help

We welcome contributors of all backgrounds and skill levels.

Web Engineering

Build frontend UI components (HTML/CSS/JS, React, Next.js), optimize performance, and maintain automated data scripts.

UI/UX & Graphics

Design responsive interfaces, accessibility improvements, infographics, and mobile user flows.

Localization (Surigaonon/Filipino)

Translate and review service guides in Surigaonon and Filipino so every Clavernon can access public information.

Content & Data Research

Verify municipal procedures, audit government publications, gather open datasets, and write clear FAQs.

Community Outreach

Spread awareness among barangays, students, local businesses, and community organizations.

QA & Testing

Test links, report bugs, verify mobile responsiveness, and maintain quality assurance across all pages.

Getting Started

3 Simple Steps to Start

Joining the BetterClaver team takes only a few minutes.

1. Explore GitHub

Check out our open-source codebase, active issues, and repository guidelines on GitHub.

2. Connect With Us

Join our Discord server or send an email to introduce yourself and share your areas of interest.

3. Pick a Task

Select an open issue, suggest an improvement, or submit your first pull request!
